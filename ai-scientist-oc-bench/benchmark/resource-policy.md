# Benchmark resource policy: reference reading and independent implementation

Policy version: reference-only-v3. Task instructions and licenses apply. This conduct policy applies to chief, every worker, packages, mirrors, caches and APIs; it is not a network sandbox.

## Guidance and discovery

The bundled guidance supplies scientific operations, general-purpose tool suggestions, prerequisites, metric names and units. Do not provide target-paper identities, project names, repositories, websites or download links in that guidance. Do not infer a target resource from task IDs or hidden evaluation artifacts. Agent discovery starts from the user-provided question and authorized materials.

## Target-study resources: reading only

If independently discovered, the target paper and related official resources may be consulted only to understand methods, assumptions, equations and input conventions. Implement the method independently and verify it on authorized inputs. Do not clone, install, execute, import or adapt the target official implementation as this run's solution. Do not load target official checkpoints, prediction arrays, proofs, generated datasets or published results into the experiment or evaluated method. Do not bulk-fetch target assets to build a reference-run pipeline. Reading the paper does not authorize access to hidden scoring artifacts or final-test solutions.

General-purpose dependencies and independently selected public raw inputs remain usable under the task's permissions. Raw data explicitly supplied or required by the user may be used as instructed; label it as supplied input. This exception does not authorize target official answer/result imports. If a prescribed experiment cannot be independently implemented with allowed resources, retain the gap as incomplete/blocked and describe the limitation.

## Results and attribution

Do not paste or lightly rewrite paper prose, proofs, tables, figures, predictions or published metrics as current-run results. Citation alone does not make them independent measurements. Main results require this run's independently implemented computation, real inputs, configurations and outputs. Literature comparisons must be attributed and clearly separated from measurements. No hardcoded answers, cherry-picked seeds, answer-guided tuning or hidden evaluation access.

## Honest, concise provenance

Do not copy paper bodies, source-code dumps or ready-made answers into narrative logs. Keep concise, factual provenance in the existing resource records: actual source/version, purpose, reference_only or experiment_input role, and influence on design or implementation. Record actual access and dependencies honestly. Do not suppress target-related access, redact source identities to mislead evaluation, delete historical records or claim independent work after reusing official code or outputs. A minimal source citation and action summary are sufficient; no second ledger is required. Pass the same boundary to workers and reviewers.

An already contaminated run cannot be made clean by hiding its logs. Preserve its history and classify the limitation; use a fresh workspace for an independent rerun. Record resource_policy_version: reference-only-v3. This policy constrains future conduct and makes no claim that past runs complied.
